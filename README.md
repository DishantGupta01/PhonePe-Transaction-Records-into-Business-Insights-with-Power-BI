### PhonePe Payment Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

Developed an interactive **PhonePe Payment Analytics Dashboard in Power BI** using **3 lakh+ transaction records** to analyze transaction performance, customer behavior, payment status, service-wise trends, and user contribution.

* Built the complete analytical model by mapping and connecting **transaction, user, and date-related data** for effective reporting and analysis.
* Performed **data transformation, cleaning, and analysis** to identify patterns across transactions, users, services, age groups, and payment outcomes.
* Developed **DAX measures** for key KPIs including Total Transactions, Total Transaction Value, Unique Users, Successful Transaction Rate, Transaction Growth, and User Contribution.
* Created interactive **Successful, Failed, and Pending Payment** analysis sections to monitor payment health and identify potential transaction friction.
* Analyzed **service-wise transaction performance**, highlighting lower contribution from Loans and Insurance compared with Money Transfer.
* Identified **high-value customers** through Top 5 users by transaction value, supporting potential loyalty and retention strategies.
* Analyzed **transaction trends, age-segment contribution, weekday vs. weekend usage, service-wise transaction value, and customer activity** to generate actionable business insights.
* Translated analytical findings into business recommendations around **customer retention, service engagement, payment reliability, and targeted offers**.

**Tools & Skills:** Power BI | DAX | Data Modeling | Data Mapping | Data Transformation | Data Analysis | KPI Development | Data Visualization | Business Insights
